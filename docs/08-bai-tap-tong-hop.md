# 08 — Bài tập tổng hợp (200 câu + đáp án)

Làm bài ra giấy trước, **rồi** mới xem đáp án ở cuối file.
Mỗi chuyên đề 20 câu. Nếu sai quá 5 câu trong một chuyên đề → quay lại học buổi tương ứng trên web.

## Chuyên đề 10 — Từ nối, mệnh đề phân từ, đảo ngữ (Buổi 32–33, 36)

1. ___ the bad weather, the match went ahead. (Although / Despite)
2. ___ it was raining, we went out. (Although / Despite)
3. The plan is risky. ___ , it could work. (However / Although)
4. Sales dropped; ___ , the company cut costs. (therefore / although)
5. ___ ___ ___ being tired, he finished the race. (mặc dù, 3 từ)
6. ___ tired, she went to bed early. (Feel → ?)
7. ___ in 1990, the bridge needs repairs. (Build → ?)
8. ___ ___ his homework, he went out. (Having + ?)
9. ___ ___ the answer, he asked for help. (Not + ?)
10. Rút gọn: Because she was invited, she came. → ___ , she came.
11. Đảo ngữ: I had never seen it. → Never ___ ___ seen it.
12. Đảo ngữ: You must not open it. → Under no circumstances ___ ___ open it.
13. Đảo ngữ: Only when he apologised ___ ___ forgive him.
14. Điền: No sooner had they left ___ it rained.
15. Điền: Hardly had I sat down ___ the phone rang.
16. Câu chẻ: I need a holiday. → ___ I need is a holiday.
17. Câu chẻ: Nam broke the window. → ___ was Nam ___ broke the window.
18. Câu nào mắc lỗi dangling participle: *Walking home, I saw a cat* / *Walking home, the rain started*?
19. Sửa lỗi: *It was late, however we stayed.*
20. Sửa lỗi: *Never I have seen such a thing.*
